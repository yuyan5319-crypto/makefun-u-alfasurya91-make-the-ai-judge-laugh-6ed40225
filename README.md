## Why this line

The joke is built on the one piece of office equipment everyone has a grudge against: the printer. Every office worker has watched a printer jam, blink, and refuse to work on the day a deadline matters — so "the printer finally worked today" lands as a small miracle the listener already believes. The turn is the reversal: the one time it works, it works against you, doing the most dramatic office act of all — quitting on your behalf, and doing it properly (printed, stapled, delivered) before you can chicken out. The setup is a true observation, the punchline is a fresh angle rather than a stock printer complaint, and the whole thing reads aloud in one breath at 123 characters.
